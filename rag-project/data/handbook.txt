PART XXXIII — THE "GOLDEN RULES" FOR BANK EMPLOYEES

If you wanted to put only one page on every employee's desk, I would make it this:

10 GOLDEN BANKING RULES

1. Verify before you process.
Never process a transaction or customer request without completing the required verification.

2. Follow the approval hierarchy.
Never bypass delegated authority.

3. Protect customer information.
Confidential information must only be accessed and shared for legitimate purposes.

4. Follow KYC/AML requirements.
Know the customer and understand unusual activity.

5. Never manipulate records.
Banking records must be accurate, complete and traceable.

6. Separate duties.
Maker, checker and approver responsibilities must be properly controlled.

7. Report suspicious activity.
Do not ignore unusual transactions, fraud indicators or control breaches.

8. Reconcile.
Cash, GL, payment systems and other relevant balances must be reconciled.

9. Follow the latest regulation.
Do not rely on an old circular when a newer NRB directive or amendment exists.

10. When uncertain, escalate.
Ask Compliance, Risk, Legal, Operations or the appropriate authorized department rather than guessing.

Key official references

For a real bank handbook, I would keep these official sources as the master references:

NRB — Unified Directives and Circulars
NRB — Banking & Financial Institution Regulation
NRB — AML/CFT / FIU
NRB — Money Laundering Prevention Supervision
Nepal Law Commission — Bank and Financial Institutions Act 2073

One important point: this should be treated as a handbook/summary, not the complete legal text. A complete bank compliance manual would need to map each rule to the specific NRB Unified Directive section, circular number, applicable Act/Regulation, responsible department, required evidence, reporting deadline, and penalty/non-compliance consequence. NRB's directives are actively amended, including during FY 2083/84, so that mapping is important for a production compliance manual.
